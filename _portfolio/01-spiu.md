The **Smart Pole Interaction Unit (SPIU)** is a pedestrian-facing roadside interface for encounters with autonomous vehicles in shared spaces. It aggregates connected-vehicle intent and presents non-binding WALK/STOP guidance intended to reduce the decision-making burden created by complex shared-space negotiations.

The program spans early concept development, immersive-VR validation, human and multimodal-LLM design evaluation, expert studies, and cross-cultural deployment across Japan and Norway.
