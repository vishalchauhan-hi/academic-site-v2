Mobile SPIU extends the infrastructure-side interface into a deployable prototype for in-the-wild research. It delivers explicit WALK/STOP guidance while moving with the experimental setting and can be studied alone or together with vehicle eHMI.

The field study involved 21 participants and crossed vehicle intent, mobility state, vehicle-eHMI presence, and SPIU presence. The work received an ACM CHI 2026 Honourable Mention Award.
