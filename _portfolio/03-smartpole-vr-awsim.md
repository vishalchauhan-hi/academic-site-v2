This research platform connects immersive VR with autonomous-driving simulation to support repeatable evaluation across road layouts, visibility conditions, and interaction scenarios. It packages scenarios, scoring criteria, and documentation into a reusable testbed for cross-study comparison.

The platform supported the Japan–Norway cross-cultural evaluation reported at ACM VRST 2025.
